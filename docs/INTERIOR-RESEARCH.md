# Interior fittings research: what makers of wardrobes and kitchens offer inside the cabinet

Date: 2026-10-23. Purpose: choose the range of interior items (step 3, bay by bay) that the Grain & Joint workshop stocks.

## How reliable this is (read first)

- 17 brands gave usable evidence (12 for wardrobes, 8 for kitchens; IKEA, Häfele and Hettich count in both). Counts below mean "brands where I found the fitting listed", not market share. A count of 5 against 6 is a tie.
- Many brand pages block fetching or are thin, so a lot comes from search snippets, retailer pages and the brand's help pages. Where a brand's own page was unreachable I say so in the sources table.
- Wardrobe fittings are counted out of 12 wardrobe brands, kitchen fittings out of 8 kitchen brands. Three wardrobe brands are thin (Tylko, Sharps, Spaceslide), so a missing item for them is "not found", not "not offered".
- Desk and wall-unit interiors: none of the surveyed brands publish such fittings. Cable tray, keyboard drawer, book shelf and media niche cannot be counted. Only IKEA's "cable management" category and Tylko's "cable ports" show up.
- Not reachable or nothing useful: Nolte (only resale listings, no fittings data), Superfront (only fronts, legs, handles, shelves and brackets, so it sells no interior fittings; confirmed from its design page), hülsta (insolvent in 2024 per Wikipedia, the interior page was found via snippets only), Tylko (HTTP 403 on fetch, snippets only), Häfele AU shop (403), Sharps interior pages (fetch returned empty, snippets only), Elfa product page (404, planning guides via snippets), Nobilia brochure PDF (over the 10 MB fetch limit, snippets only), California Closets finish page and Pickawood (partly via snippets). Blum and Hettich consumer pages gave product families but few options. The Hettich colour/finish details come from Hettich's own shop and India pages plus retailers.
- Extra, not counted: Diotti, Salice (wardrobe hardware), Victory Closets, Closet Parts (US closet makers) appeared in results and are used only to back up an option.

## Recommendations

### (a) Keep (offered by 3 or more brands)

shelf (5), glass shelf as a material option of shelf (2, plus Diotti), long rail (9), pull-down rail (4), drawer stack (10), inner drawers (behind the door; same family, 10), jewellery drawer (5), divided drawer (4), angled shoe rack (6), pull-out shoe rack (8), pull-out trouser rack (9), tie and belt rack (7), pull-out basket (6 wardrobe, 4 kitchen), laundry pull-out (4), mirror (5), accessory hooks (5), LED strip (6), vertical divider (2, but it is structural and cheap, so keep). Kitchen: cutlery insert (5), pan drawer (4), bin pull-out (3), corner carousel (6). Valet rod (3) is borderline: keep, it is a cheap part and Hettich plus two US makers sell it.

Merge:
- Short rail into long rail with a length parameter (no brand sells it as a separate item).
- Double hanging into two rails stacked (1 brand names it; keep it as a preset only).
- Socks/underwear divided drawer into "drawer with divider insert" (4 wardrobe plus 3 kitchen).

### (c) Drop as rare

- Fold-out ironing board (1 of 12).
- Cubby grid as its own item (2 of 12); bays with dividers and shelves already make one.
- Desk and wall-unit items cannot be judged from this survey. Keep cable tray and keyboard drawer (needed physically), keep book shelf (it is a shelf) and media niche (a bay with no door).

### (e) Per-bay options

- Interior colour: default "same as carcass" (matches `interior: null` in the contract). Offer 3-4 neutrals as an override: white, oak, grey-beige, dark grey (IKEA has 4, Elfa 2, Hammonds one). Pickawood's 14 decors are more than the market usually expects.
- Back panel: enclosed (standard) or open; mirror back panel (Neatsmith). Colour follows the interior colour of the bay; a contrast colour is a luxury (not seen at any brand except as "match the doors", Spaceslide).
- Note: the contract's interior setting is per piece (`interior: { material, finish }`). A per-bay override is new and needs a CONTRACTS.md change first.

## Open questions

- Hettich, Blum and Kesseböhmer consumer pages are trade-oriented. Check an actual workshop supplier before stocking runners and lifts.
- Check Tylko and Sharps directly: their interior lists are almost empty here because of blocked fetches.
- Colour names for linings are my summary of "three neutral colours"; California Closets does not publish the names in the pages I could read.
