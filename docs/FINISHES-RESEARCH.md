# Finishes research: what makers of custom cabinetry offer most often

Date: 2026-10-23. Purpose: choose a small stock range of finishes for the Grain & Joint configurator.

## How reliable this is (read first)

- 15 brands gave usable information. Most brand home pages are thin or block fetching (HTTP 403), so a lot comes from search snippets, help-centre pages, press pages and trade articles. Counts are "brands where I found evidence", not exact. Treat a count of 5 vs 6 as a tie.
- Hex values are my estimates from colour names. Only a few brands publish hex or NCS codes. Check against real samples before you fix them.
- Some brands are British or Danish kitchen makers, some are IKEA-front makers, some are shelving makers. All sell made-to-measure fronts or cabinetry online or by quote. They show what customers expect to be able to choose.
- Not reachable / nothing found: Form Kitchens (DNS error, no search hits), Uncommon Projects (no hits), Koak Design (no hits; replaced by Baboon Amsterdam), Schrankwerk / deinSchrank (no hits). Tylko, Howdens and Dezeen returned 403 when fetched directly, so their data comes from search snippets. Nordiska Kök was not found separately; Husk is only a single project page.

## Per-brand lists

**1. Pickawood.** Solid woods (oiled): oak, wild oak, cherry, heartwood beech (11 species claimed, 4 named). Lacquered MDF: RAL 9005 black, pastel blue, silk grey, pebble grey. Coated decors: black, white, white-grey, grey, cream, basalt grey, oak. Real veneer: oak, oiled. Doors: lacquered, sliding, mirror, glass, coloured. Handles: 5 styles in different materials or colours, or push-to-open. Worktops: table tops exist, no options found. Plywood: multiplex edge option.

**2. Plykea.** Birch plywood (FSC). Veneers: oak, ash, walnut, maple. Finish: Osmo hard wax oil. Laminates: Fenix, Arpa, Formica (colours not named). Handles: brass pulls, round and rectangular cut-outs, quarter/half arc grab handles, brass edge pulls. Worktops: birch ply with Formica. Fronts: flat doors, drawer fronts, cover panels, spacer panels.

**3. Superfront.** Lacquer colours: Desert Sand, Silent Greige, Almost White, Super White, Willow Green, Aerugo Green, Concrete, plus custom NCS. Colour categories on site: beige, white, grey, blue, black, green. Wood veneer: Honey, Biscotti, Umber, Obsidian, Willow, Alabaster. Handle finishes: silver, black, brass, wood (also chrome, stainless, aluminium listed as pairing). Handle types: sideboard, wardrobe, drawer handles, knobs. Designs: Plain, Vertical, Harlequin, Geometric, Blocks, Golden (veneer only on Plain and Vertical). Stone top panels.

**4. Reform.** Painted (two-component lacquer, 25 gloss): 18 colours, names not found; custom NCS for a fee. Linoleum (Forbo): 13-24 colours; found Vapour, Mushroom, Powder, Conifer, Pistachio, Pebble, Ash. Veneer: natural oak, smoked oak. Handle: one round 44 mm disc in natural oak, smoked oak or painted oak. Worktops: linoleum, solid wood, stainless steel; Corian/GetaCore per Gessato. Designs: BASIS (slab with round pull), PLAIN, SHAKER.

**5. Tylko.** Classic: 19 mm birch plywood, matt, oiled edges. Classic Veneer: oak, white oak, walnut. Modern: 11 colours: White, Terracotta, Midnight Blue, Sand + Midnight Blue, Matt Black, Sky Blue, Cotton Beige, Grey, Reisinger Pink, Sage Green, Stone Gray. Handles: colour-matched aluminium (ply), solid wood (veneer). No glass found.

**6. deVOL.** 16 Shaker paints: Linen, Quaker Cream, Minton Buff, Scullery Yellow, Damask, Mushroom, Princelet Pink, Refectory Red, Trinity Blue, Clerkenwell Blue, Bond Street Blue, Pantry Blue, Folgate Green, Wilkes Green, Bakehouse Green, Printer's Black. Bespoke matching extra. Handles: Bella Brass knobs, cup handles, pulls. Worktops: Carrara marble, limestone, granite. Design: Shaker.

**7. Plain English.** 60 paint colours in 6 collections (names not published in text; Collection 5 is greens, 6 is bold/coastal). Worktops: honed marble, oiled timber (teak). Doors: Shaker, Long House, Spitalfields, Folgate, Wilkes. Handles: leather seen once.

**8. Naked Kitchens.** Own palette: Misted Fen (grey-green), Pamment (muted pink-brown), Marsh Green (dark green), Bramble (purple), plus colour matching. Worktops: marble-effect quartz, white marble, black. Brass accents. Doors: Shaker, Ladbroke. Naked Doors: painted, whitewashed or exposed oak or walnut.

**9. Howdens.** Greenwich slab 16 mm: navy, marine blue, dove grey, croft grey, reed green, oak, whites. Hockley: matt black, mirror gloss, textures (18 mm slab). Shaker: Allendale, Frome In-Frame, Chilcomb (sage green), Elmbridge. Handleless versions of Greenwich. Handle and worktop lists not found (wood worktops shown).

### Plywood (1)

### Laminate / linoleum (2, optional)
Fenix-type matt laminate in Black and Light Grey (Fenix in 3 brands, coloured laminate in 7). Skip linoleum: only Reform offers it.

### Glass (2)
Clear glass and Grey mirror glass (grey-tinted). Evidence is 3 brands for glass doors and 2 for mirror. Treat as a judgement call.

### Worktops (4)
1. Solid oak (4 brands).
2. Light stone or quartz, marble look (5).
3. Dark stone, honed black (3).
4. Matt laminate/HPL (3). Stainless steel (1) is not worth stocking.

### Handle finishes (4)
Brass (5), Oak wood (5), Black (4), Brushed steel (4).

### Handle styles (5)
Bar pull (6), Round knob (5), Edge or finger pull routed into the front (5), Cup pull (deVOL, Plykea arc: 2, supports the traditional look), Push-to-open / handleless (4).

### Front designs (4)
1. Flat slab (12). Staple.
2. Shaker (8). Staple.
3. Slim frame with glass insert (Semihandmade, Pickawood, IKEA doors: 3). Uses the two glass types.
4. Ribbed / vertical groove (2). Trend: offer only if you can make it cheaply, or leave it out.

## Staple vs trend

- Staples (found in many brands for years): off-white, grey, greige, black, sage/muted green, natural oak, slab and Shaker fronts, brass and black handles, stone worktops.
- Trends: terracotta/clay, blush pink (Tylko's Reisinger Pink is a designer collaboration), aubergine, ribbed fronts, smoked oak, Fenix in strong colours, linoleum, scalloped or edge-cut handles.
- Fading: high-gloss (IKEA Fardal, Howdens mirror gloss still sold but not growing in this sample); nothing here proves a decline, it is just no longer the headline.
