Process

Five steps from first call to the last screw.

No surprises: you see the drawing, the 3D model and the price before a single board is cut, and you hear from us every week while it is being built.

5 to 12 weeks from signed design to fitting

  1. 01Week 0

    Call & visit

    We start with a conversation and a tape measure.

    A 30-minute call to hear what the room needs to do. Then one of us visits with samples, looks at the light, opens the cupboards you already have and notes what stays.

    • Free call and visit, no obligation
    • Sample boards and hardware to handle
    • Photographs and a first sketch on the day

    You getA written price range within 48 hours

  2. 02Week 1

    Measure (laser-scanned)

    Every wall, to the millimetre.

    Walls are never straight and floors never level. We scan the room with a laser, capturing sockets, pipes, skirting and the slope of the ceiling, so panels are scribed to the real room rather than the plan.

    • Laser scan of walls, floor and ceiling
    • Services, radiators and sockets mapped
    • Scan-accurate drawing sent to you

    You getA drawing accurate to 1 mm

  3. 03Weeks 1 to 2

    Design & 3D

    See it before we cut it.

    We model your room and the furniture in 3D, then walk you through it. You choose boards, fronts and handles from real samples. When you are happy we fix the price and the date.

    • 3D model you can orbit and share
    • Materials and handles chosen from samples
    • Fixed quote and a booked workshop slot

    You getA fixed quote and your 3D design

    Try the configurator

  4. 04Weeks 2 to 8

    Workshop

    Cut, joined and finished at Timber Lane.

    Boards are cut from the cut list, joined with dovetails, tenons and dowels, glued and clamped, then sanded and oiled or lacquered. Everything is dry-assembled before it leaves the workshop.

    • Cut list nested to reduce waste
    • Joints cut and fitted by hand
    • Dry-fitted and photographed before delivery

    You getProgress photos every week

  5. 05Last week

    Fitting

    Two days, one tidy room.

    We deliver, level and scribe the furniture into place, hang the doors, adjust every hinge and runner, and take every offcut and dust sheet away with us.

    • Scribed to walls, floor and ceiling
    • Doors and drawers adjusted until even
    • Room left clean, care guide handed over

    You getA two-year workmanship guarantee

Joinery explained

How the joints go together.

Tails on one board, sockets on the other. The wedge shape locks the corner so the joint can only come apart the way it went together. We use it for drawers and carcass corners.

A tongue cut on the rail fits a slot cut in the post, then is glued. It is the strongest way to join a frame, which is why we use it on table legs and frames.

  1. Cut the pieces
  2. Line up the joint
  3. Slide it home
  4. Glue and clamp

Scroll to slide the joint together, or drag the slider. Drag the model to turn it.

The 3D view needs WebGL, which is not available in this browser. A dovetail locks two boards at a corner with interlocking wedges; a mortise and tenon fits a tongue into a slot.
