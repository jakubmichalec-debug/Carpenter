Process

How it works

You see the drawings, a 3D model and the price before we cut anything, and we send photos every week while it’s being built.

5 to 12 weeks from signed design to fitting

  1. 01Week 0

    Call & visit

    A call, then a visit.

    A 30-minute call to hear what the room needs to do. Then one of us visits with samples, looks at the light, opens the cupboards you already have and notes what stays.

    • Free call and visit, no obligation
    • Sample boards and hardware to handle
    • Photographs and a first sketch on the day

    You getA written price range within 48 hours

    Walnut top, sage lacquer · Kontich

  2. 02Week 1

    Measure (laser-scanned)

    We scan the room.

    Walls are rarely straight and floors are rarely level. A laser scan records the sockets, pipes, skirting and any slope, and we scribe the panels to those measurements.

    • Laser scan of walls, floor and ceiling
    • Services, radiators and sockets mapped
    • Scan-accurate drawing sent to you

    You getA drawing accurate to 1 mm

  3. 03Weeks 1 to 2

    Design & 3D

    You approve a 3D model and a fixed price.

    We model your room and the furniture in 3D, then walk you through it. You choose boards, fronts and handles from real samples. Once you’re happy, we fix the price and the date.

    • 3D model you can orbit and share
    • Materials and handles chosen from samples
    • Fixed quote and a booked workshop slot

    You getA fixed quote and your 3D design

    Try the planner

  4. 04Weeks 2 to 8

    Workshop

    Built in our workshop.

    Boards are cut from the cut list, joined with dovetails, tenons and dowels, glued and clamped, then sanded and oiled or lacquered. Everything is dry-assembled before it leaves the workshop.

    • Cut list nested to reduce waste
    • Joints cut and fitted by hand
    • Dry-fitted and photographed before delivery

    You getProgress photos every week

    Beech workbench, hand tools · Timber Lane

  5. 05Last week

    Fitting

    We fit it, usually in a day.

    We deliver, level and scribe the furniture into place, hang the doors, adjust every hinge and runner, and take every offcut and dust sheet away with us.

    • Scribed to walls, floor and ceiling
    • Doors and drawers adjusted until even
    • Room left clean, care guide handed over

    You getA ten-year written guarantee

    Oak interior, soft-close drawers

  6. 066 weeks after fitting, then yearly

    Aftercare

    We come back once the timber has settled.

    Wood moves with the seasons. Six weeks after fitting we return to re-adjust doors, drawers and runners, touch up the finish and answer anything that came up while you lived with it. Our ten-year guarantee covers every joint and hinge.

    • Free adjustment visit at six weeks
    • Care guide and a pot of your finish
    • One phone number for anything that comes up

    You getA ten-year guarantee, in writing

    Solid oak, hand-softened edge

Questions

Questions

Still wondering about something? Ask us directly.

Most commissions take five to twelve weeks from signed design to fitting. Wardrobes and desks sit at the short end, kitchens at the long end. The quote states the exact lead time, and we hold a workshop slot for you once you approve the design.

The call and the visit are free. A €250 design fee covers the laser scan, the 3D model and the fixed quote. It’s part of the quote total, so you don’t pay it twice if you go ahead.

They rarely are, which is why we laser-scan: panels are scribed to the actual wall, floor and ceiling, and any remaining gap is closed with a shadow line or a scribed return, not a ribbon of filler.

Until we cut the first board, yes, at no cost. After that changes are priced at cost. Fronts, handles and interior fittings are the easiest to change late; overall sizes are the hardest.

We coordinate with your electrician and plumber, leave the cut-outs and supply LED strips and drivers. The mains connection is always made by a certified electrician, ours or yours.

Boards by area, hardware by count, workshop hours and installation, plus the design fee. The planner uses exactly the same table, so the estimate you see there is the starting point for our fixed quote.
