Materials

What we offer

A small, curated range: every paint, veneer, board, glass, worktop and handle finish we stock, and nothing else. Open any one for a bigger photo and its price. The planner uses this same list.

The catalogue needs JavaScript. We offer eight paint colours, four wood veneers, birch plywood, two laminates, two glasses, four worktops and four handle finishes. See them in the planner.

Photos show the look of each finish, not an exact colour match. Paints are one plaster photo tinted per colour, and the laminates are matte stand-in surfaces. Prices are material prices per m² from the planner's table; your quote adds the build and fitting. A sample box is the only true colour check.
