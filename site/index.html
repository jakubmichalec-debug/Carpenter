How it works

From tape measure to fitted.

Five steps, one team, and a fixed price before we cut a single board. Then we stay in touch.

Keep scrolling →

01

Measure

We visit, scan every wall and note the sockets, skirting and the slope of your floor. Rooms are never square, so we do not pretend they are.

Laser-scanned · 2 mm tolerance

02

Design

You see your room in 3D and change doors, shelves and timber until it feels right. The configurator gives a live price and a cut list as you go.

3D proposal · fixed quote

03

Make

Boards are cut, jointed and glued in our Antwerp workshop. Every drawer is dovetailed, every door is hung and adjusted before it leaves the building.

Built in-house · 4 to 8 weeks

04

Fit

The same people who built it install it, usually in one day, and leave the room cleaner than they found it. A ten-year guarantee comes with the keys.

Fitted in 1 day · 10-year guarantee

05

Aftercare

We return after six weeks to adjust doors and runners as the timber settles, and send a care guide for your finish. Anything that needs attention, we fix.

Visit at 6 weeks · care guide
