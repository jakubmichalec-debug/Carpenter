How it works

How a project runs

You get a fixed price before we cut anything. Most projects take 5 to 12 weeks from signed design to fitting.

Keep scrolling →

01

Measure

We come round and laser-scan the walls, floor and ceiling, including sockets, skirting and how much the floor slopes.

Laser-scanned · 2 mm tolerance

02

Design

We model the room in 3D and you choose doors, shelves and wood. The planner shows the price as you change things.

3D proposal · fixed quote

03

Make

We cut, joint and glue everything in our Antwerp workshop, and hang and adjust the doors there before anything is delivered.

Built in-house · 4 to 8 weeks

04

Fit

The people who built it install it, usually in one day (two for kitchens), and take the offcuts and dust sheets with them.

Fitted in 1 day · 10-year guarantee

05

Aftercare

Wood moves a little in the first weeks, so we come back after six weeks to adjust doors and runners. If something needs fixing later, call us.

Visit at 6 weeks · care guide
